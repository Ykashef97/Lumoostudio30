HOW TO CHANGE A PHOTO ON THE WEBSITE
====================================

1. Open IMAGE-NAMES.txt (in this folder) and find the photo you want to replace.
   Example: the "Pleated shade arm" wall light is called  sconces-02

2. Rename your new photo to that name and put it in this folder.
   Example:  sconces-02.jpg
   JPG, PNG and WEBP all work. You can add several photos at once.

3. Go back to the main project folder and double-click  update-images.bat
   It resizes the photo and puts it in the right place.
   The first time, it installs a small free image tool (needs internet).

4. Double-click  preview.bat  to see the website on this computer.
   If the old photo still shows, press Ctrl+F5 in the browser.

5. The website on the internet changes only after the files are published
   (see "How to preview and publish" in the LumaDesign Website Guide).

Your original photo is kept in the "done" folder here, with the date in its name.


TIPS FOR GOOD PHOTOS
--------------------
- Lights: plain background, the lamp in the middle with some space around it.
  Wall and ceiling lights look best as tall photos; chandeliers as square photos.
- Room photos: wide (landscape), at least 1600 pixels wide. Keep the main subject
  on the right side, because the headline text sits on the left.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO
---------------------
It replaces existing photos. It does not add a new product or rename one.
For that, the product list in  assets/js/app.js  has to be edited.


NEEDS
-----
Python (free, from python.org) installed on this computer.
