About LumaDesign

Light, shaped around you

The right light starts with your room, not with what happens to be on a shelf. LumaDesign brings design, preview and personal advice together, so the light you choose fits the way you live.

Explore the collection

Our philosophy

Every room has its own story

A bedroom wants a softer glow than a dining table. A home office needs clear, calm light. We start from the space and the people in it, then help you find or shape the light that suits them.

That is why LumaDesign is built around three simple ideas: see the options clearly, shape them to your taste, and see them in your own room before you decide.

Light should feel as if it was chosen for the room, because it was.
