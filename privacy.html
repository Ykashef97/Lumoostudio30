Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This page explains what personal details LumaDesign collects through this website, why, where they are kept and how you can see, correct or delete them. We collect as little as we need to help you choose and order lighting.

What we collect, and why

When you book a consultation

The form asks for your name and phone number, and optionally your email address, how you prefer to be contacted, a preferred day and time, and any notes. If you start from a design, the design and the size, finish and cord you chose are attached. We use these details only to contact you, arrange the consultation and prepare a quote.

When you request a quote

The quote form asks for your name and phone number, and optionally your email address, how you prefer to be contacted and any notes. The pieces in your selection are sent with it, including the size, finish and cord of a lamp you designed. We use these details only to contact you with the price, lead time and delivery details.

If you choose to send the list on WhatsApp instead, WhatsApp opens with a message already written. Nothing is sent to us until you press send there, and the message is then handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy terms.

Hearts on designs

When you tap the heart on a design, we add one to an anonymous count for that design, so we know which designs are popular. The count is not linked to you, your device or any personal detail.

Room photos and room designs

Photos you add in “Try in your room” are processed entirely in your browser, and rooms you save are kept only on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

If you choose to send your room design with a consultation request and leave “Attach the picture of my room design” ticked, we receive one picture of your design (your room photo with the lights you placed) and the list of lights. It is stored privately with your request and only our team can see it. Untick the box if you prefer to send only the list of lights.

What stays on your device

Your shortlist, your selection, saved lamp designs and the name, phone and email you last typed into the consultation form are stored in your own browser, so you do not lose them between visits. They are not sent to us. A copy of the website's content is also kept in your browser for a few minutes so pages load faster. You can remove all of this at any time by clearing this website's data in your browser settings.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or visitor analytics.

Where your details are kept

Consultation and quote requests are stored in a secure database run by our hosting provider, Supabase. Only LumaDesign's admin account can read them; they are never shown on the website. If we keep a spreadsheet copy to organise requests, it is stored in our own Google account.

Like any website, the services that deliver this site (GitHub Pages for the pages, Google Fonts for the typefaces and jsDelivr for some code libraries) receive your device's internet address when your browser downloads files from them. We do not receive or store this information.

Who we share them with

We do not sell or rent your details, and we do not share them with anyone for marketing. They are only handled by the providers named above, who store them for us, and by the people at LumaDesign who answer your request.

How long we keep them

We keep a consultation or quote request for as long as we need it to help you, prepare and follow up your order, and keep our own records. When you ask us to delete it, we do so unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to show you the details we hold about you, to correct them, or to delete them, and you can ask us to stop contacting you. Contact us using the details on the Contact page. You also have the right to complain to the Personal Data Protection Center in Egypt.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal details, we will update this page and the date at the top.
